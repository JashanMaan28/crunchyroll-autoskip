Skip the parts you’ve already seen

Autoskip skips the recap, the opening, the end credits and the next-episode preview on Crunchyroll. A button fills up for 3 seconds, then it jumps. Press Esc to watch instead.

Install for Chrome Try it in your browser

Also for Edge and Firefox. Free and open source.

One episode, four skips

The same 24-minute episode, laid out. The orange parts repeat every week, and Autoskip jumps over each one the moment it starts, using the episode’s own skip timestamps.

Stays for the scene after the credits

Some episodes hide a scene after the ending. When there are at least 10 seconds of footage after the credits, Autoskip skips the credits and lets the scene play. When there’s nothing, it goes straight to the next episode.

Your show, your rules

Keep the openings you love
Open the popup on any episode and switch a part from Skip to Watch. It sticks for every episode of that show.
Hear each new opening once
The opening plays the first time you watch a show and on every episode 1. After that it’s skipped.
Mark your own skips
Press AltShiftM where a part starts and again where it ends. An eyecatch, a recap in the middle, a post-credits scene you don’t want: it’s skipped on every episode of the show.

The small things

Pause freezes it
Pausing stops the countdown where it is. It picks up when you press play.
Rewind brings it back
Seek back into the opening and the button comes back, in case you want to hear it again.
Skips on the seek bar
The parts it will skip are shaded on the player’s progress bar.
Binge limit
After a number of episodes in a row, it pauses and asks if you’re still watching.
Sleep timer
Pause after 15 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour, or at the end of the episode.
Weekly recap
Time saved this week, a bar per day, and the shows you watched most.

Enter skips now, Esc watches instead, AltShiftS turns it on or off. Read the guide

Nothing leaves your browser

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your settings and stats are kept in your browser. The only request Autoskip makes is for the episode’s skip timestamps, from Crunchyroll. Read the privacy policy

Install

The Chrome Web Store listing is in review. Until it’s approved, install it from the release on GitHub. It takes a minute.

Chrome and Edge

  1. Download crunchyroll-autoskip-chrome-%VERSION%.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the orange icon so the settings are one click away.
Download for Chrome

Firefox 128 or newer

  1. Download crunchyroll-autoskip-firefox-%VERSION%.zip.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick the zip.
  4. Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it restarts. The README explains how to keep it.

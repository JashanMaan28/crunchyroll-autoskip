Guide

Everything Autoskip does, and how to change it. Most of it works without touching a setting.

Getting started

Install the extension (see Install), open any episode on crunchyroll.com and press play. There’s nothing to set up. Click the orange icon in the toolbar to see what Autoskip found in the episode you’re watching and to change the settings. Pin it so it’s one click away.

Autoskip uses the skip timestamps Crunchyroll publishes for each episode, the same ones its own Skip Intro button uses. When an episode doesn’t have them yet, which happens with some brand-new simulcasts, Autoskip waits for the player’s own skip button and presses it for you.

The countdown

When a recap, opening, ending or preview starts, a button appears at the bottom right of the player and fills up over 3 seconds. When it’s full, Autoskip skips.

  • Skip right away: click the button or press Enter.
  • Watch it this time: click ✕ or press Esc. That part plays, and it stays cancelled for this episode.
  • Pausing freezes the countdown where it is. It picks up again when you press play.
  • Seeking back before a part brings the button back, so you can rewatch the opening and still have it skipped.

The parts that will be skipped are shaded on the player’s progress bar. You can turn that off under Show skips on the seek bar.

Credits and the next episode

With Smart next episode on (the default), the ending works like this:

  • If there are at least 10 seconds of footage after the credits, Autoskip skips the credits and lets that scene play. When it ends, it goes to the next episode.
  • If there’s nothing after the credits, it goes straight to the next episode.
  • The next-episode preview counts as nothing, unless you’ve told it to play previews.

With Smart next episode off, Autoskip skips the credits and stops there, and Crunchyroll’s own autoplay takes over.

Per-show rules

Open the popup on an episode. It lists the parts it found, with their times. Switch any of them from Skip to Watch and it sticks for every episode of that show. Use this for the openings you never want to miss. The rules are listed under Per-show rules in the popup, where you can remove them.

To stop skipping a kind of part everywhere, for example previews, turn it off under Skip in the popup’s settings.

New openings

With Play new openings once on, the opening plays the first time you watch a show and on every episode 1, then it’s skipped. If the page shows the season number, a new season’s opening plays once too. The popup shows Once next to an opening that’s going to play. Click it to skip that one as well.

Your own skips

For anything Crunchyroll doesn’t mark, like an eyecatch, a recap halfway through, or a post-credits scene you don’t care about:

  • While watching: press AltShiftM where the part starts, then again where it ends. Esc cancels.
  • From the popup: click Mark a part to skip and type the start and end. Now fills in the current time, and End runs it to the end of the episode.
  • Skip the scene after the credits: in the popup, switch After credits to Skip. From then on the credits go straight to the next episode for that show.

A mark applies to every episode of the show. Autoskip saves it relative to the nearest point it knows (the end of the credits, the start of the preview, the end of the episode), so it lands in the same place even when episodes differ in length. Pick This episode in the popup for a one-off.

Binge limit and sleep timer

Binge limit: after the number of episodes you choose in a row, Autoskip pauses and asks if you’re still watching. Picking an episode yourself resets the count.

Sleep timer: pause after 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour, or at the end of the current episode. Set it from the popup.

Weekly recap: the popup shows the time saved this week, a bar per day, and the shows you watched most. The stats stay in your browser, and Reset clears them.

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysWhat it does
EnterSkip now, while the button is showing
EscWatch this part instead
AltShiftSTurn auto-skip on or off
AltShiftMMark the start, then the end, of a part to skip

Change the last two at chrome://extensions/shortcuts, or in Firefox at about:addons, then the gear menu and Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Questions

Does it work in the Crunchyroll app?

No. Phone, TV and console apps can’t run browser extensions. It works on crunchyroll.com in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer.

An episode isn’t being skipped

Some episodes, especially ones that just aired, don’t have skip timestamps yet. Autoskip then reacts to Crunchyroll’s own skip button when it appears. Check the popup on that episode: if it lists no parts, there’s no data for it yet.

Esc also leaves fullscreen

In fullscreen, Chrome uses Esc to exit fullscreen and pages can’t stop that. Click ✕ on the button instead if you want to stay in fullscreen.

Does it collect anything?

No. Settings and stats are stored in your browser, and the only request Autoskip makes is for the episode’s skip timestamps, from Crunchyroll. See the privacy policy.

Something’s broken

Crunchyroll sometimes changes its page, which can stop Autoskip from finding the player. Open an issue with the episode’s link. To include the details, open DevTools on the episode, go to the Console, turn on the Verbose level and filter for CR Autoskip.
